            Classification inheritance rule
            
A derived artifact must inherit the most restrictive classification of its source documents. A CONFIDENTIAL source always yields a CONFIDENTIAL artifact — downgrade requires an explicitly authorized declassification process.

Simple Security Property (ss-property)

              

                S may read O  ⟺  Clearance(S) ≥ Classification(O)
              

              

                A subject at a given security clearance level may not read an object at a strictly higher classification level ("No Read Up"). Untrusted agents cannot inspect confidential company secrets.
