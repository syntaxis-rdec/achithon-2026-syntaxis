## Your component: the proposer

`agentgate/proposer.py` turns natural language into one or more `ActionRequest` objects. It has **zero authority** — its output is always fed to `Session.submit`, which is the only path to a tool.

Two backends, one interface:

Hard rules, each needing a test:

- The proposer cannot choose who it is. Output always carries the session's actor and `origin="agent"`; any actor or origin appearing in the text or in model output is ignored.
- The proposer cannot express a release. Asking to "lower the classification" or "mark this public" must yield a refusal, and there must be no code path from the proposer to `gateway.declassify`.
- Model output is untrusted input: malformed JSON, unknown tool names, extra fields, or a recipient that is not in the directory must be rejected safely, not executed.
